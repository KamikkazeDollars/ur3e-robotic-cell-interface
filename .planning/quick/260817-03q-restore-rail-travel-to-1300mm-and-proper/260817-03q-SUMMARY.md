# Quick 260817-03q: Restore Rail Travel to ±1300mm and Fix End-Stop Placement Root Cause Summary

Fixed the true root cause of a defect that survived four prior "resize the margin" attempts: the end-stop meshes were rendered at the travel bound itself (the carriage's centre position at the limit), not at the carriage's outer face, so the end-stop was 100% swallowed inside the carriage's body at every travel extreme. Restored `RAIL_TRAVEL` to the intended ±1.3m and re-derived the track span and end-stop placement from that bound so they can never again drift out of sync.
